MZT Player 1.4.0

The current tape encoder is implemented from the documented Sharp MZ PWM
protocol. No third-party tape converter source or executable is included or
required to build, test or run this version. See docs/tape-format.md in the
source repository for the protocol references and implementation choices.

The Sharp MZ ship emblem is based on Sharp's historical Argo mark. The mark
belongs to Sharp. Its use here does not imply endorsement by Sharp.
